Headphones
Aether Tochilovsky
My lucky charm is my headphones. I never go anywhere without them, and I feel like they keep me “protected” in a way. I have noise sensitivities and I am always listening to music or using the noise cancelling function. I really love listening to new music, and I feel more understood than ever when I hear a song that reminds me of a situation, a person, a memory, or myself. If I forget my headphones, it’s almost a guarantee that I’m gonna have a hard time wherever I am, so they are my “lucky charm” (although for a more logical, less magical reason).
