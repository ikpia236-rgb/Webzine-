One weird ritual that I have is whenever the clock strikes 11:11, I have to touch something red and make a wish. I don’t really remember when I started doing this or where I learned it but it has become something I automatically do whenever I notice the time. The rule is that I have to find something red, touch it, and keep touching it until the minute changes to 11:12; During that minute, I have to think of a wish that I want to come true. It can sometimes be funny because if I'm somewhere where there isn’t anything red nearby, I start to look around for anything that could count as red. I could be someone's clothing, a random object, etc. I know there isn’t any actual proof of doing this will make my wishes come true but I still feel like I have to do it just in case. I think that’s what makes superstitions so interesting. Even when we know they might not mean anything, we still follow them because they give us a sense of good luck.
